# Zanskar

> Zanskar (Zanskar Geothermal & Minerals, Inc.) is a Salt Lake City-based geothermal energy company founded in 2021 that uses custom artificial intelligence, machine learning, and subsurface geoscience to discover and develop conventional geothermal resources. Backed by Obvious Ventures and Union Square Ventures. Zanskar publishes public geothermal datasets on the Snowflake Marketplace; it does not currently expose a public developer API.
